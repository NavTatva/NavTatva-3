A short field guide

How to read NavTatva.

  1. Choose an observation

    Each scene is a real Sentinel-1 radar image. The date is when the satellite observed the water, not today's date.

  2. Run the analysis

    The detector looks for oil-like dark areas. Wind and current data estimate a release zone and where the slick may travel next.

  3. Follow the map

    Orange outlines show detected oil; the red zone estimates its origin. Blue marks the forecast. Toggle layers to isolate evidence, and use the timeline to replay available vessel positions.

  4. Inspect candidates

    Vessels are ranked by location, timing, movement, type and behaviour. The score is an investigation priority out of 100, not a probability of guilt. Open a vessel to see its evidence and track.

  5. Read the limitations

    Simulated traffic is labelled. Missing AIS means attribution is unavailable; clean water means there is no detected slick to attribute. Download the report to keep the results and their limitations together.
